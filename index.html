GIS coursework Spring 2025

Mapping Hurricane Harvey’s Urban Rainfall Extremes

I automated PRISM precipitation downloads with Python, clipped August 2017 monthly rasters to Texas and Harris County, and used county-level zonal summaries to explore spatial variation.

Cartographic methods

I applied cubic resampling and Jenks classification for display. August 2017 monthly totals include rainfall outside Hurricane Harvey; resampling changes presentation, not measurement resolution.

CSUN Geography 490 capstone Spring 2025

Rethinking Wildfire Risk

Hydroclimate whiplash, urban growth, and dynamic fire hazard mapping in Los Angeles County

I created thematic maps in ArcGIS Pro comparing the Eaton and Palisades fire perimeters with shrub/scrub land cover and CAL FIRE hazard zones, supported by a Los Angeles rainfall chart.

Methods and source notes

I clipped NLCD shrub/scrub land cover (class 52) to Los Angeles County and used Raster Calculator to create a binary raster. I overlaid CAL FIRE and LA County GIS layers, assembled map layouts, and visualized LA Almanac rainfall records in Excel.

These are descriptive overlays; terrain, wind exposure and fuel moisture were not modeled.
